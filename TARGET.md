## In one sentence

Heads, directors and VPs of Revenue Operations, Sales Operations or GTM Operations at US B2B software companies of 51 to 500 employees.

## What fits

- **Role:** owns revenue, sales or go-to-market operations, at Head, Director or VP level.
- **Company:** B2B software, 51 to 500 employees.
- **Place:** United States.

## What does not fit

- Consultants, advisors, fractional and independent roles: they do not buy for a company.
- "Sales & Operations" titles outside software (manufacturing, distribution, services).
- Sales roles without operations in the title.
- Companies over 500 employees.

## Rules for the search

- **Precision target: 80%.** At least 20 of the 25 previewed leads must fit before any export.
- **Three rounds at most.** If the third round is still under the target, show the misses and ask what to change.
- **Leads per run:** 20.
- **Every week:** a recurring export returns only the people who are new to the search.
